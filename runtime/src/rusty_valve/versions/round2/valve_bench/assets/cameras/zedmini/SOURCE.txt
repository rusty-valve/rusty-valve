STL units are metres; do not recenter it. Original mesh bounds are approximately
X [-.00769,.02181], Y [-.05483,.07037], Z [-.01334,.01330].
The official URDF uses a camera-center frame with +X forward, +Y left, +Z up;
left/right optical centers are Y=+/- .0315 m. The camera-center is .01325 m
above the bottom mount reference. Our adapter uses a project-designed head
post, rotates the manufacturer's body frame into MuJoCo's camera axes, and
keeps the source optical centers. It does not claim the post is official CAD.
Own camera housing is hidden only in its optical image to represent the
through-lens optical path; robot/tool/environment occlusion stays visible.

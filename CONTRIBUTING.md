# Share a valve station

Share a valve you least want to operate in a chemical plant. Give its nominal size or model, and use photos or videos to explain the work, adding text only for what the images cannot show.

## Submission form

### 1. Nominal size / model (required)

For example: DN25 ball valve.

### 2. Photos, videos, or a written description (required)

Add photos, videos, sketches, or links showing where the valve is, how people operate it, and what makes the work difficult or dangerous. No need to repeat what the media already shows; if you have no media, describe the station in words.

### Before submitting

- [ ] I can share this content publicly and have removed plant names, equipment tags, and faces.

Submissions are discussed publicly on GitHub, so prepare your media before attaching it. You do not need to leave a phone number or email address; we can follow up in the submission thread.

Submitting photos or videos does not require releasing them under CC BY. For use in other project presentations or open datasets, we will separately agree on the use, attribution, and license.

## Where to submit

In this repository, select **Issues → New issue → Share a valve station**. Each submission becomes an issue where you can view the material and discuss it with others.

## What happens next

We will discuss which operations could become robot tasks. A complex station may be split into independent actions; once a task is defined, we can gradually add field references, simulation environments, demonstrations, and test methods.
